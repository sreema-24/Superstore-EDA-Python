# Superstore Sales EDA — Python

## Project Overview
Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on 4 years of US retail sales data using Python. Analyzed sales trends, regional performance, product profitability, and customer segments to uncover key business insights.

## Tools Used
- Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn)
- Google Colab
- Dataset: Superstore Sales Data (Kaggle)

## Analysis Performed
- Dataset overview and missing value check
- Total Sales and Profit by Category
- Sales by Region
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Sales vs Profit correlation
- Sales by Customer Segment

## Key Insights
- Total Revenue: $2.30M
- Profit Margin: 12.47%
- Technology is the highest revenue category
- West region leads in total sales
- Consumer segment drives 50%+ of total sales
- Canon imageCLASS 2200 is the top selling product
